How the Production Counter Works

Four simple steps, no sign-up and nothing to install.

1

Open the counter

Open the production counter on your phone, tablet or computer. The big tap area and the current count are ready at once.

2

Tap once per item

Each tap adds exactly one to the total. Keep a steady rhythm and let the counter hold the number for you.

3

Fix mistakes at once

Tapped twice by accident? The minus button steps back one count, so a small slip never ruins the total.

4

Read and reset

Read the total when you finish, note it down, and reset to zero for the next round, batch or shift.

About the Production Counter

The production counter counts finished units in real time. Tap once per item as it passes quality control, and the total shows the shift's output at a glance. Track the rate per hour, compare shifts, and spot slowdowns early — all from a phone or tablet on the line.

If you need a plain, dependable place to keep that number, a production counter is a practical choice for everyday counting. It does not replace your judgement — it simply holds the count steady while you focus on the task itself.

Tips for Accurate Counting Every Time

Small habits make the difference between a trusted total and a recount.

Tap once per finished unit, at the same point in the process every time.
Reset the counter at the start of each shift, not in the middle.
Note the hourly rate, not just the total, to spot slowdowns early.
Keep a second count for rejects so the good total stays clean.
